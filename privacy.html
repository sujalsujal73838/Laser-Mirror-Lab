01 · Welcome

What is Laser Mirror Lab? 🔬

Laser Mirror Lab is a science-themed Android puzzle game created by Sujal M. Players explore light, mirrors, beam splitters and curved mirrors by solving interactive challenges.

This policy explains what the game stores on your device, what advertising services may process, and how to reach the developer with privacy questions.

02 · Game data

Your game progress stays in the game ⭐

The game does not require an account or sign-in. It does not ask you to enter your name, email address, date of birth, photos, contacts or precise location to play.

The game stores these types of information locally using the device's Android WebView storage:

🏆 Progress and rewards

Completed levels, stars, coins, unlocked chapters, cosmetic items and achievement-related records.

🧪 Experiments and preferences

Saved experiments, sound and vibration settings, tutorial completion and gameplay statistics.

The game does not send these saved puzzle records to a developer-operated server. This is separate from the technical information that Google AdMob may process for advertising.

☁️ About Android backups
Android backup is enabled in the app's configuration. Depending on your device and backup settings, saved app data may also be included in your device's Google-managed backup. Removing the game may not immediately remove a previously created backup.
03 · Advertisements

How ads work 📺

Laser Mirror Lab uses Google AdMob to help support free gameplay. The game can show banner ads on selected non-gameplay screens, interstitial ads at eligible transitions between levels, and optional rewarded ads that grant in-game coins.

The game's advertising configuration includes a starting grace period before ads can appear, and it does not intentionally display banner ads over the active puzzle board.

What information could Google process?

To provide, measure, secure and troubleshoot advertisements, Google and its service providers may process information such as an IP address, general device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostics. Some information may be used for approximate location or fraud prevention.

The app's reviewed AdMob integration requests child-directed treatment, treatment for users under the age of consent, a maximum G ad content rating, and non-personalized ads. Its Android manifest removes the Advertising ID permission. These settings do not mean Google processes no data.

See How Google uses information from partner apps, Google Privacy Policy, and AdMob child-directed ad information.

04 · Children and families

Made with younger learners in mind 🌈

Laser Mirror Lab is designed as an educational game for school-age learners and families. It does not require children to provide a name, email address or birth date to play, and it does not include player-to-player chat or social profiles.

The reviewed Android build applies child-directed and non-personalized advertising settings for its ad requests, rather than asking children to create a marketing profile. Third-party advertising processing is described in the section above.

Parents and guardians can contact sujalamansuri@gmail.com about privacy questions. The app's settings route external privacy links through a parental-check step.

05 · Choices and retention

You're in control of your progress ⚙️

  • Reset progress: In the game, open Settings and use Reset Progress. This clears locally saved gameplay progress, rewards and experiments; some preferences are kept, as described by the game.
  • Remove the app: Uninstalling generally removes locally stored app data from the device. Android backups, if enabled, may be retained separately under your Google backup settings.
  • Ads: Rewarded ads are optional. Depending on your location and applicable law, Google or the app may offer additional privacy or consent choices.
  • Privacy questions: Contact the developer using the email address below. For information processed by Google, also refer to Google's privacy policies and controls.

Local game data is retained on your device until it is cleared, removed, or otherwise deleted by the operating system. Third-party ad-data retention is governed by the applicable provider's practices.

Permissions used by the Android app

Internet and network status: ad delivery and checking connectivity. Vibration: optional touch feedback. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise-location permissions in the reviewed Android manifest.

06 · Quick answers

Frequently asked questions 💬

Do I need to create an account?

No. Laser Mirror Lab does not require sign-in or a player profile.

Does Sujal M receive my level progress?

The game stores your progress on your device and does not upload those puzzle records to a developer-operated server. Google advertising services separately process technical ad-related information.

Why do I see ads?

Ads help support the free game. Rewarded ads are optional and can provide in-game coins. Other ad formats may appear at selected screens or level transitions.

Can I delete my progress?

Yes. Use Settings → Reset Progress in the game. If your device backs up app data, you may also need to manage that backup separately in Android or your Google account.

Does the game know my exact location?

The reviewed app does not request precise location permission. However, an advertising provider may infer an approximate area from network information such as an IP address.
